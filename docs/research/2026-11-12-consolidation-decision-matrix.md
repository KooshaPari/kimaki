Date: 2026-11-12. **Provisional recommendations, not approval to migrate, merge, deploy or retire.** M7 remains open: parity, data preservation, rollback rehearsal and owner approval are not proven. [L]

## Evidence and terminology

- [I] [Bounded existing-project inventory](2026-11-11-existing-projects.md), especially its evidence ledger, preservation matrix, license caveats and migration gates. Observations are source-level, dated 2026-11-11, not verification of current deployments, contents of production stores or ownership.
- [L] [Closure ledger](../unified-agent-ops-closure-ledger.md), milestone status and observed interface limits. [B] [Backend adapters](2026-11-11-backend-adapters.md). [H] [herdr research](2026-11-11-herdr-session-binding.md). [P] [Paperclip research](2026-11-11-paperclip-integration.md).
- **Coexist** means retain the existing authority or service and federate only where qualified. **Migrate** means transfer a specified capability and its state after parity and rollback proof. **Reverse-merge** means move Kimaki or its operations layer into the candidate, making the candidate the consolidation host. Neither implies authorization. All recommendations below are provisional; unknown is not absent.
- Named accountable human owners, production operators, deployed instances, data volumes and current usage are **unverified** for every row. `KooshaPari` in a repository locator is not proof of operational ownership. Identify the actual owners before action. Data entries describe evidenced data types or explicitly flagged possibilities, not inspected production contents.

## Required gates before any cutover

1. Identify named owners and actual consumers, locate complete repositories without disturbing active writers, record revisions and licenses, and inventory data, secrets handling, jobs and retention through approved redacted means. No approval is presumed here. [I]
2. For each proposed migration, compare unique workflows and mappings, prove export/import integrity and retention, perform authorization and tenant-isolation checks, and obtain affected data and service owner sign-off. In particular preserve PhenoBot pending receipts and mappings. [I]
3. Run an owner-approved canary and rollback rehearsal with backup and reconciliation of pending work. Demonstrate native-session non-ownership and no accidental replay or duplicate side effects. Any missing parity, unresolved data or failed rollback blocks retirement. [I, L]
4. Obtain explicit final retirement approval from each service owner and affected data/consumer owners after the parity and rollback report. Separate approval is required for license/provenance, security/privacy and any publication of private topology. No legacy service was operated or altered for this document.
